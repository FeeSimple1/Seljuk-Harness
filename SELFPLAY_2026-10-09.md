# Seljuk: deliberately played self-play game and follow-up regressions

## Game record

- Scenario: **Manzikert**; seed **20261009**; standard rules, no optional rules enabled.
- Source at start: `3be117b100b9691619fc189f749235afa937eaca`.
- **132 successful player actions**, chosen for both sides through `LLMSession` rather than a random-action policy.
- The game began in Spring 1071 (calendar 10), played Spring and Summer Campaigns, and ended at the start of the Autumn 1071 Campaign (calendar 12), after Autumn Levy.
- **Seljuk victory under rule 5.2: Romans had no Mustered Lords during Campaign.** Displayed score: Roman **1**, Seljuk **3.5**. The no-Lords condition, not the VP comparison, decided the game.
- The saved replay includes each action, a brief rationale, its result, and before/after state hashes. All 132 states match on replay, including a save/load after every action. The complete legal game gives exactly the same states before and after these fixes.

This is one deliberately played test game, not a statistical balance study or a game between independent, information-isolated agents. Strategic actions and selected combat decisions were supplied explicitly; unspecified internal combat choices used the engine's deterministic fallback. No Winter occurred in this trajectory. Summer Heat and Winter budget handling were checked separately with focused tests.

## What happened

**Spring:** Alp Arslan's army supplied and ravaged outside Aleppo, then captured it in a two-round Storm. The Roman force moved east from Constantinople, recruiting two infantry Themata and later paying a Coin to retain the Sebasteia markers. Sav-Tekin left his siege of Theodosiopolis rather than face the approaching Roman army; the abandoned investment cleared correctly.

**Summer:** The Romans invested Manzikert with Sav-Tekin inside. Sanduq arrived to relieve him. This produced a three-round Battle with a Relief Sally, a Roman rearguard, Themata strikes, concession, and retreat. The Romans won that Battle, but subsequent food shortages and Service expiry disbanded Romanos, Roussel, and Sanduq. These were adverse play outcomes, not confirmed defects.

Meanwhile, the southern Seljuk army advanced on Edessa. The Roman garrison received Themata defenders and used R4 Sultan's Horse to reduce the Siege. A one-round Storm nevertheless succeeded; the Seljuks chose to ruin Edessa. The remaining Roman force stopped at Melitene when it lacked the actions to cross the Pass. Its Lords then left during the Autumn Levy, ending Roman resistance.

## Confirmed findings

### SP-01 — entire-card commands accepted on a partially spent card

After Alp Arslan supplied outside Aleppo, **three of four actions remained**. The raw and validated menus still offered Siege, and a direct submission on a discarded copy succeeded. Neighboring copies of Romanos's Ankyra position demonstrated the same problem with Tax and a Holding-Box March: after Supply, Tax succeeded and the trip to Constantinople was charged only the three remaining actions.

Repository authority: `source/rules.txt`, **4.2.1**, **4.3.3**, **4.5.1**, and **4.5.6**. These distinguish commands costing the *entire* card from a Battle, Storm, or starting a Siege merely *ending* the remaining actions.

**Correction:** Raw menus and direct handlers share an entire-card budget check. The original activation budget, including Capability modifiers, is retained rather than recomputed after movement or rerolled. Rejection precedes asset discards, the Peace Offering Coin charge, dice rolls, and other state changes. Ordinary affordable Marches and Storms remain legal after earlier actions. Summer Heat replaces the fresh card's budget with one; Winter activations initialize and clear their own budget.

The exploit was tested on copies only and was **not used in the recorded game**. The entire replay therefore remains legal and unchanged after correction.

### SP-02 — duplicate Marwanid Alliance menu entry

At Autumn Call to Arms, with one Coin on S8, the menu listed the identical concrete action to activate Amid twice. The maximum-affordable selection overlapped with the single-Seat selection. The same duplication occurs when only one Seat remains available.

**Correction:** Offer the combined selection only when it contains both Seats; offer each affordable, eligible single-Seat choice once. Tests cover insufficient Coins, one/two Coins, an already-active Seat, and Ruins. No legal choice is removed.

### SP-03 — Summer Heat can be submitted outside its response window

This was found in **follow-up tests of the SP-01 correction**, not during the
recorded game. After declining Summer Heat and spending a Supply action, a
direct `play_hold_event` submission still accepted R3/S4. The same handler also
accepted the event against the holder's own Lord. The menu correctly omitted
these plays, but the handler did not enforce the enemy-reveal window.

**Correction:** Require the matching pending enemy-reveal response, the opposing
side, and the unspent activation budget before applying or consuming the event.
This also prevents a late event from resetting a partly spent card to a new
full Command-1 budget. Both R3 and S4 are covered for late, declined, and
own-Lord submissions; valid in-window play remains covered.

## Non-bugs and coverage limits

Four submitted player instructions were correctly rejected without changing any state: naming a Road where the connection is a Pass, Foraging in a Ravaged Locale, attempting to act for the next side too early, and attempting a two-action Pass with only one action remaining. These are recorded separately and are not counted as engine bugs.

Every successful player action was checked for state invariants and JSON round-trip stability. No invariant failure or uncaught engine exception occurred during the accepted game sequence. That does not establish correctness of all positions, all combat-choice combinations, or all rules.

## Regression test changes

The new file adds **39 cases**: the complete replay, reproductions, direct-action
and menu checks, and neighboring legal controls. On the original source, **29
fail and 10 pass**; some failures deliberately require the corrected activation
budget interfaces. On the corrected source, all 39 pass.

Two existing positive-test setups were corrected without removing their
assertions: Honors of War now receives Romanos's full four-action card rather
than three actions, and Summer Heat opens the actual enemy-reveal response
window. The normal test suite retains every original test case.

Run the focused replay and regressions with:

```bash
python -m pytest -q tests/test_selfplay_20261009.py
```
