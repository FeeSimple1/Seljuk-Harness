================================================================
SELJUK — LORDS REFERENCE
================================================================
Compiled from physical mats (verified by user) and cross-checked
against the Rulebook, Playbook, and Errata & Clarifications.
 
Notation:
  "?"   on a Seat indicates a conditional Seat activated only
        when a Capability is in play (Marwanid Alliance for
        Amid/Mayyafariqin; Roman alignment for Arisighi's
        Constantinople Holding Box).
  Service Rating (vassals) is used only with optional rule 6.2;
        ignored in standard play.

ROMANOS DIOGENES — "Emperor of the Romans"
----------------------------------------------------------------
Commander (tall cylinder; always Roman Commander when on map)
Fealty: 6   Service: 4   Lordship: 3   Command: 4
Seat: Constantinople
 
Starting Forces:    2x Tagmata, 1x Infantry, 2x Turkic Horse
Starting Assets:    2 Carts, 2 Provender, 2 Coin
 
Vassals:
  - 2x Tagmata, 2x Infantry, Service 3
  - Special: Emperor's Retinue (red cross background)
             1x Varangian Guard, 1x Hetaireia, Service 1
             (Requires R7 Capability; Romanos only)
  - Special: Scholai (red cross background)
             1x Hetaireia, Service 1
             (Requires R17 Capability; shared with Andronikos)
 
Notes:
  - As Commander, may pay Coin from his mat for any Unbesieged
    Roman Lord regardless of distance (rule 1.5.2 SHARING).
  - Can lead Group March (4.3.1) and Recruit Themata (4.5.7).
  - Can Tax Friendly Strongholds in the Roman Empire that are
    not his Seat (4.5.6).
  - Eligible for R9 Provincial Bureaucracy (Commander-only).

================================================================
SPECIAL VASSALS
================================================================
 
The game has exactly four Special Vassals, identified by
distinctive borders and background symbols on their markers
(rule 1.5.1). All four start the game SET ASIDE and only enter
play when their associated Capability has been Levied (rule
3.4.2). Discard of the Capability card Disbands the Special
Vassal.
 
Mustering a Special Vassal is a two-step process:
  1. Spend Lordship to Levy the Capability card and place the
     Special Vassal marker in the Lord's Assets section.
  2. Spend an additional Lordship on a later Levy (or same Levy
     if Lordship permits) to actually Muster the Vassal,
     adding its units to the Lord's Forces.
