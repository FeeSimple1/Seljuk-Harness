================================================================
SELJUK - THEMATA SCENARIO REFERENCE
================================================================
 
Combines the master Themata Service Marker roster (from the
user's physical-counter inventory, cross-checked against the
rulebook) with the per-scenario "Remove from Play" lists and
face-up map placements for all five published scenarios.
 
SOURCES
  - Master roster: user-provided marker inventory (May 2026).
  - Removal lists, Calendar data, face-up placements: rulebook
    scenario pages 26-35 and the printed scenario-setup map
    illustrations.
  - Cross-checks: rulebook "Remove from Play" text, playbook
    examples, project Map Reference.
 
PRECEDENCE
  Where this file and the Rules of Play or the Errata &
  Clarifications differ, the Rules and the Errata govern.
 
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
MARKER NOTATION
----------------------------------------------------------------
 
Each line in a Thema's roster is ONE Themata Service Marker.
 
  "1 Infantry"     single-symbol Infantry marker
  "2 Infantry"     one marker depicting 2 Infantry symbols
                   ("double infantry" in scenario text). Rolls
                   one Protection die for Ravage and is Lost on
                   a failed roll; deploys 2 Infantry units as
                   Garrison in Storm. (Rules 1.5.1)
  "1 Tagmata"      single-symbol Tagmata marker
  "1 Militia"      single-symbol Militia marker
  "1 Turkic Horse" single-symbol Turkic Horse marker
 
Themata are Roman-side only. They are placed in their Thema box
on the map at setup, and both players may inspect a Thema box's
contents at any time -- there is no face-down / hidden state for
markers sitting in their home box (Rules 1.5.1). The counter
back simply shows the Thema name as a placement reminder.
 
"Face-up on the map" in the scenario sections below refers to a
marker the scenario-setup illustration shows displayed at a
specific Stronghold Locale rather than tucked in the Thema box.
This is a setup/visual convention; rules-wise the marker still
belongs to its Thema.
 
Paphlagonia is omitted from all rosters: its only Locale is the
Constantinople Holding Box, and no scenario references a
Paphlagonia Themata marker.
 
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
VERIFICATION STATUS KEY
----------------------------------------------------------------
 
  [rulebook]   The marker is named explicitly in rulebook
               scenario "Remove from Play" text, so its
               existence and type are confirmed.
  [inventory]  The marker comes from the user's physical-counter
               reading. No scenario thins this marker, so the
               rulebook offers no cross-check. Treated as accurate.
 
  The full roster below is treated as accurate. Themata Garrison
  markers are drawn only from Infantry, double Infantry, Tagmata,
  Militia, and Turkic Horse types; Scholai/Hetaireia and Norman
  Knights are Lord Forces and Vassal units, never Themata, so
  they would not appear in a Thema box. Turkic Horse is the only
  mounted Themata type (confirmed at Iberia), which settles the
  Melitene mounted marker as a Turkic Horse.

CONTENT NOTES
  - Iberia Turkic Horse: confirmed. Four scenarios name
    "1x Turkic Horse" when thinning Iberia (Specter of Norman
    Betrayal, Year of Treacherous Ambition, Showdown in
    Anatolia, Manzikert).
  - Melitene Militia and Turkic Horse: the scenarios that thin
    Melitene name only Tagmata and double-Infantry removals, so
    these two markers come from the physical-counter reading.
    Treated as accurate -- Turkic Horse is the only mounted
    Themata type, and no non-Themata unit could sit in a Thema
    box.
  - Sebasteia 2-Infantry and Tagmata, and Armeniakon's 3rd
    marker (the 2nd 1-Infantry): from the physical-counter
    reading. The playbook Reset example (p. 10) confirms
    Sebasteia holds at least a Tagmata + Infantry, consistent
    with this roster.
  - Boukellarion: spelled with a k (not "Boubellarion"). No
    scenario thins it, so both markers are [inventory].

1068 A.D. Campaign Scenario
================================================================

  Charsianon: 1 Tagmata

  Charsianon  -> 1 Militia        (Tagmata removed)
  All other Themata: full 1068 baseline roster, in their boxes.

  illustration shows Charsianon's lone Militia displayed at a
  Stronghold rather than in the Thema box, note it here.

================================================================
CONSISTENCY NOTES ACROSS SCENARIOS
================================================================
 
  - Charsianon's Tagmata is removed in every scenario. Its
    Militia survives in Emperor and the Lion, Specter, Year of
    Treacherous Ambition, and Showdown, but is also removed in
    Manzikert (leaving Charsianon empty there).
 
  - Iberia is emptied (both markers removed) in every scenario
    except Emperor and the Lion, where Iberia keeps its full
    baseline pair.
 
  - Melitene's Militia and Turkic Horse are present in Specter,
    Year of Treacherous Ambition, Showdown, and Manzikert --
    those scenarios only remove Melitene's Tagmata and/or double
    Infantry, leaving Militia + Turkic Horse as the box contents.
 
  - The face-up convention (Melitene Turkic Horse, Sebasteia
    Tagmata, and the sole-remaining markers of single-marker
    Themata) is taken from the scenario-setup map illustrations
    and from the user's reading of those illustrations. The
    rulebook scenario TEXT does not specify face-up placement;
    only the printed map shows it.
