================================================================
SELJUK — SEQUENCE OF PLAY REFERENCE
================================================================
Source: Rules of Play sections 2.0-4.7 and Player Aid Card,
cross-checked against Seljuk_Errata_and_Clarifications.txt.
Errata corrections applied inline.
 
PRECEDENCE
  Where this file and the Rules of Play or the Errata &
  Clarifications differ, the Rules and the Errata govern.
 
CONVENTIONS
  Player order is Seljuk first, then Roman, unless stated
  otherwise (2.2.4).
  "Turn" = one Seasonal Turn (90 days / one Calendar box).
  Each Turn consists of one Levy sequence followed by one
  Campaign sequence.
  Winter is a non-campaign interphase conducted after Reset
  in the first three Autumn turns only (Calendar boxes 3, 6, 9).
 
 
================================================================
A. LEVY (3.0)
================================================================
Conduct at the outset of each Seasonal Turn.
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
A.1  ARTS OF WAR (3.1)
----------------------------------------------------------------
A.1.1  Shuffle (3.1.1)
  Collect and shuffle all unused Arts of War cards into each
  side's Event draw deck. Held Events (3.1.3) and Capabilities
  already in play (3.4.4) are NOT included in the draw deck.
 
A.1.2  First Levy of scenario — Draw Capabilities (3.1.2)
  Seljuk then Roman each draw 2 Arts of War cards and deploy
  them as Capabilities (lower half of card, 1.9.1) at that
  side's map edge or Lord mats (1.5.2).
  Return to deck any "This Lord" card that cannot be assigned
  to a Mustered Lord (3.4.4).
 
A.1.3  Second or later Levy — Draw Events (3.1.3)
  Seljuk then Roman each draw 2 Arts of War cards and
  implement their Events (upper half) in the order drawn.
  Reveal Events immediately unless card text says otherwise.
  Event subtypes:
    - Normal Events: resolve immediately, return card to deck.
    - "Hold" Events: keep hidden for later use (e.g., in Battle).
    - "This Campaign" Events: remain in effect until end of
      current Campaign (do not return until Reset, 4.7.5).
      NOTE: The Capability on a "This Campaign" Event card
      is not available for selection this Levy.
    - Asterisk (*) Events: once per game. Mark the current
      Season on the Calendar with the reminder marker.
  NOTE: A side's Events can affect the opposing side. The
  side playing the Event makes any decisions unless otherwise
  specified.
 
A.1.4  Greed (3.1.4)
  Players may not voluntarily discard cards unless permitted
  by a rule. Hold Events return to deck only when used or
  discarded at Campaign's end (4.7.5).
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
A.2  PAY (3.2)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Seljuk then Roman may expend Coin and Loot to shift Service
Markers right (later) on the Calendar. Coin and Loot may only
be removed in this step to shift Service Markers (1.7.2).
 
A.2.1  Pay with Coin (3.2.1)
  Any Lord with Coin on his mat may remove some or all Coin
  to shift Service Markers right by one Calendar box per Coin:
    - The paying Lord's own Service Marker, OR
    - That of another Lord at the same Locale.
  EXCEPTION: Unbesieged Commanders in play may pay Coin
  regardless of location for any Unbesieged friendly Lord.
 
A.2.2  Pay with Loot (3.2.2)
  Any Lord at a Friendly Locale free of Siege (may be
  Bypassed) may pay with Loot from his mat (or Share from
  a Lord at the same Locale) as if Coin, one for one.
  IMPORTANT: Must be at a Friendly Locale free of Siege.
  May do so at an Unbesieged Stronghold with that side's
  Conquered markers, but NOT at a Besieged Locale.
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
A.3  DISBAND (3.3)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Seljuk then Roman check for Lord Disbanding.
 
A.3.1  Beyond Service Limit (3.3.1)
  For each Lord whose Service Marker is to the LEFT of the
  current Levy/Campaign marker (in a lower-numbered Turn
  box), remove him from the map.
  REMOVAL: Permanently remove cylinder(s) from play. Set
  aside mat, Service Marker, Vassal Service Markers, Forces,
  and Assets. Return "This Lord" Capability cards (3.4.4) to
  the Arts of War deck.
  If the Lord was Seljuk and had a Roman Strategic Objective
  marker, the Roman player claims it in the Constantinople
  Holding Box.
 
A.3.2  At Service Limit (3.3.2)
  Lords with Service Markers in the SAME Turn box as the
  Levy/Campaign marker Disband but may Muster again later.
  - Place cylinder on the Calendar, a number of Seasonal Turn
    boxes to the RIGHT equal to the Lord's Service Rating
    (1.5.3). If beyond box 12, place off the board (2.2.3).
  - Return Forces and Assets to pools. Return Capability cards
    under the mat to the Arts of War deck. Set aside mat(s),
    Service Marker, and remaining Vassal Service Markers.
  CLARIFICATION (Errata): If the Roman Commander Disbands
  while Themata Service Markers are on his Lord mat, those
  Themata are returned to their Thema box.
  NOTE: Rarely, a Lord may Disband because his last unit is
  removed outside of combat (1.6).
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
A.4  MUSTER (3.4)
----------------------------------------------------------------
After Disband, Seljuk then Roman Lords already on the map
may take Levy actions up to their Lordship Rating (1.5.3) to
Muster other Lords, Vassals, Transport, or Capabilities.
Each Levy action spends one point of Lordship. Complete each
Lord's actions before the next Lord, in any order desired.
 
REQUIREMENTS: A Lord must have begun this Muster segment
at an Unbesieged Friendly Locale (1.3.1) including a Holding
Box. Lords may NOT conduct Muster from Enemy Locales or
at a Siege. Lords brought onto the map during this Muster
segment (3.4.1) may not take Levy actions themselves until
that Levy's Call to Arms at the earliest (3.5).
 
A.4.1  Levy Other Lords (3.4.1)
  Cost: 1 Lordship per attempt.
  Designate a Lord to roll Fealty for another Lord. The rolling
  Lord must be Ready (cylinder in a Turn box at or left of the
  Levy marker, 2.2) and must have a Seat free of enemy Lords
  or Conquered markers (may be Bypassed or Ravaged, 4.5.5).
  Roll one die; if within the target Lord's Fealty Rating (1.5.3),
  Muster him. If not, do nothing (may retry with more Lordship).
  NOTE: Lords can Levy a Ready Commander like any other Lord.
 
  MUSTERING procedure:
    - Place Lord's cylinder at one of his free Seats (or for
      Arisighi, Robert, or Roussel only, his associated Holding
      Box per 1.4.2 if Seat is enemy-occupied/Conquered).
    - Take Lord's mat; place starting Forces from pool (1.6).
    - Place Assets and Vassal Service Markers (Heraldry up,
      Ready to Muster, 3.4.2) onto Lord's mat. Keep aside any
      Special Vassal Service Markers not yet available (1.5.1,
      1.9.1, 3.4.2).
      NOTE: Do NOT place Vassal Forces on the mat when the
      Lord Musters; they are available for the Lord to Levy at
      a later time.
    - Place Lord's Service Marker on the Calendar, a number
      of boxes to the RIGHT of the current Levy box equal to
      his Service Rating (1.5.3). If beyond 12, place off-board
      (2.2.3).
 
A.4.2  Levy Vassals (3.4.2)
  Cost: 1 Lordship.
  Vassal must have a face-up Service Marker in the Lord's
  Assets & Vassals section. Slide it to Forces section. Place
  the units shown on the marker from the pool (1.6).
  NOTE: Ignore Vassal Service Ratings unless using optional
  rule 6.2.
  SPECIAL VASSALS (Scholai, Emperor's Retinue, Elite Ghulam
  Cavalry, Oghuz Mercenaries): start set aside until the
  associated Capability enables Levy. Discard of the card
  Disbands the Vassal.
 
A.4.3  Levy Transport (3.4.3)
  Cost: 1 Lordship.
  Lord at a Friendly Locale adds one Cart to his mat.
 
A.4.4  Levy Capabilities (3.4.4)
  Cost: 1 Lordship per card selected.
  Lord at a Friendly Locale obtains Capabilities (bottom half
  of Arts of War cards). Select from any of the side's currently
  unused cards, within restrictions:
    - Heraldry shows which Lords can Levy the card and whom
      it affects ("This Lord", "any", "ALL", "NOT").
    - "This Lord" cards: place at bottom edge of that Lord's
      mat. Max two "This Lord" Capabilities per Lord mat, no
      duplicates. Drawing a third forces immediate discard of
      one.
    - "ALL" / non-"This Lord" cards: tuck upper half under
      the player's closest game board edge (2.1.1). A Lord may
      Levy any number of such Capabilities (see also 4.0).
  NOTE: Levying a Capability blocks its Event from occurring.
 
A.4.5  Levy Themata (3.4.5)
  Cost: 1 Lordship (spent by Roman Commander only).
  During Muster, the Roman Commander may Levy Themata
  Service Markers as if performing a Recruit action (4.5.7) in
  his current Thema. Place the marker on the Lord's mat and
  stack the depicted unit(s) on it.
  NOTE: This differs from Vassal handling in this game and
  previous Levy & Campaign volumes.
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
A.5  CALL TO ARMS (3.5)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Each side has different strategic options depending on Levied
Capabilities. Each Call to Arms phase, Seljuks may complete
any one option, then Romans do so.
NOTE: Events can trigger at most one Call to Arms per Levy.
 
A.5.1  Capability-driven options (3.5.1)
  MARWANID ALLIANCE: Seljuk player may transfer 1 Coin
  from Alp Arslan's Assets to this card OR spend any number
  of Coin from the Marwanid Alliance card; for each Coin
  spent, make either Amid or Mayyafariqin a Seat for Seljuk
  Lords until the next Winter Phase. Lords may not Tax in
  these Locales and only one may serve as a Supply Source per
  Command card. Place Lord Seat marker(s) as reminder.
 
  EMPRESS EUDOKIA MAKREMBOLITISSA (3.5.1.2): Roman
  player may either place the Empress token on this card OR
  move it from this card to Constantinople to choose one of:
    - Shift a Roman Lord cylinder 1 Calendar box.
    - Decrease service of a Lord with a Seat at Constantinople
      1 Calendar box to increase service of Romanos Diogenes
      by 1 Calendar box.
 
  DEEP RAIDS: A Lord meeting criteria on the card may
  execute the Call to Arms action for the turn.
 
A.5.2  Loot (3.5.2)
  If desired, Seljuk player may remove a Loot marker from the
  Mosul and Baghdad box (adjusting VPs) to shift a Seljuk Lord
  cylinder 1 Calendar box.
 
A.5.3  Strategic Objective (3.5.3)
  [ERRATA APPLIED — PAC correction]
  If desired, and if a Roman Commander is in Constantinople,
  the Roman player may:
    - Take a Strategic Objective marker from the supply and
      make it available in front of them, OR
    - If they already have one or more available Strategic
      Objective markers in front of them from a previous Call
      to Arms, place one on a Mustered enemy Lord mat or an
      Enemy Stronghold in the Sultanate (1.3.1).
  May NOT take from supply AND place in the same Call to
  Arms. Three markers exist; Roman player may hold all three.
  Once claimed for VPs (3.3.1, 4.5.1, 4.9.1), the marker goes
  permanently to the Constantinople Holding Box.
 
 
================================================================
B. CAMPAIGN (4.0)
================================================================
After Levy, conduct the Turn's Seasonal Campaign.
 
CAPABILITY DISCARD: Players (Seljuk first) must select and
discard any Capability cards they have exceeding their number
of Mustered Lords — not counting "This Lord" Capabilities
(3.4.4). Compare the number of non-"This Lord" cards under
a side's map edge to its number of Lord mats in use; discard
any excess.
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
B.1  PLAN (4.1)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Each side builds a Campaign Plan: an ordered stack of Command
cards (1.9.2). May designate Lieutenants (4.1.3).
 
B.1.1  Selecting Cards (4.1.1)
  Use any cards available in that side's Command deck. Action
  cards (with a numeral) and No Command cards always
  available.
  Plan stack size by Season:
    - Spring: 7 cards
    - Summer: 8 cards
    - Autumn: 7 cards
  NOTE: Some Events may add more cards.
 
B.1.2  Arranging Stacks (4.1.2)
  Stack the required number of cards face down in any order.
  Top face-down card = first Active Lord. May not rearrange
  once built. Players may always inspect their own Plan and
  the other side's played (but not unused) Command cards.
 
B.1.3  Lieutenants (4.1.3)
  During Planning only, players may stack a Lord cylinder atop
  another at the same Locale. Upper = Lieutenant, Lower =
  Lower Lord. Lieutenant oversees Lower Lord for entire
  Campaign unless one is removed. Max one Lower Lord per
  Lieutenant. Lower Lord may not be a Lieutenant. Commanders
  may not be Lieutenants or Lower Lords. A side may have
  several Lieutenants.
  Lieutenant and Lower Lord always move together (March,
  Avoid, Retreat, etc.).
  If either Disbands, the remaining Lord becomes a normal Lord.
  Revealing a Lower Lord's Command card results in a Pass.
  A Lieutenant and Lower Lord may not Withdraw into a Fort
  (Size 1).
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
B.2  COMMAND (4.2)
----------------------------------------------------------------
After building Plan stacks, Seljuk then Roman alternate
flipping (revealing) the top Command card — one Seljuk, then
one Roman, and so on.
 
B.2.1  Actions (4.2.1)
  Lord on a revealed Command card may execute a series of
  Commands up to his Command Rating (1.5.3), in any order.
  EXCEPTIONS: Besieged Lords (4.3.5) may only Sally (4.5.3),
  Pass (4.5.8), or perhaps Forage (4.5.4). Siege and Tax use
  an entire card (4.5.1, 4.5.6). Starting a Siege and any Battle
  or Storm end that card's actions (4.3.5, 4.8, 4.9).
  NOTE: Lords may use multiple actions on a single Command
  type (e.g., Forage three times).
 
B.2.2  Command Menu (4.2.2)
  Available Commands: March, Siege, Storm, Sally, Supply,
  Forage, Ravage, Tax, or Pass. Romans can Recruit (4.5.7).
 
B.2.3  No Command Card (4.2.3)
  Revealed No Command card, a Lower Lord's card (4.1.3), or
  the card of a Lord not on the map: that side does nothing.
  Play passes to the other side, or ends the Campaign if
  neither side has cards left in its Plan (4.7).
 
  NOTE: After each Command card, Lords who moved and/or
  fought will Feed their Forces, and Lords may Pay or might
  Disband (4.6).
 
  [COMMANDS 4.3-4.5 — March, Supply, Siege, Storm, Sally,
  Forage, Ravage, Tax, Recruit, Pass — are detailed in the
  Rules of Play sections 4.3 through 4.5.8. See also the
  Commands side of the Player Aid Card.]
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
B.3  FEED — PAY — DISBAND (4.6)
----------------------------------------------------------------
At the end of EACH Command card, both sides execute:
 
B.3.1  Feed (4.6.1)
  Each Lord who Moved or Fought (Seljuk then Roman) must
  remove Provender or Loot per the number of units on his mat:
    - 1-8 units: 1 Provender or Loot
    - 9-12 units: 2
    - 13-16 units: 3
    - 17+ units: 4
  GREED: Feed may not remove Assets beyond this requirement
  (1.7.2). NOTE: Loot Pays Lords only at Friendly Locales
  (3.2.2) but Feeds them anywhere.
  SHARING: All Lords at a Locale must first Feed their own
  Forces from their own mats. Then Lords must expend
  Provender and Loot to Feed co-located Friendly Lords who
  did not fully Feed. A side may not withhold.
 
  UNFED: Shift Service Marker left one Seasonal box for each
  Lord that received less Provender or Loot than required.
  (If using Vassal Service optional rule 6.2, also shift Vassals.)
  A Lord requiring 2 or 3 but with access to fewer consumes
  them and still suffers the shift.
 
B.3.2  Pay and Disband (4.6.2)
  Any Seljuk then Roman Lords may receive Pay as per Levy
  (3.2). Then all Lords on both sides Disband as required by
  their Service limit (3.3.1-.2).
 
B.3.3  Remove Markers (4.6.3)
  Remove Moved/Fought markers from all Lords. Proceed with
  the other side's next Command card, if any.
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
B.4  END CAMPAIGN (4.7)
----------------------------------------------------------------
After revealing all Command cards in both sides' Plan stacks,
end this Seasonal Turn.
 
B.4.1  Game End (4.7.1)
  If this was the scenario's final Seasonal Turn, the game ends.
  Side with the most VPs wins (5.3). If tied, draw.
 
B.4.2  Grow (4.7.2)
  At the end of each SPRING Turn (Calendar boxes 1, 4, 7, 10):
  Seljuk then Roman each must select and reduce the Enemy's
  Ravaged markers on the map to half their total number,
  rounded up (adjust VPs, 5.1).
 
B.4.3  Repair (4.7.3)
  At the end of each Campaign: remove one Siege marker from
  each Stronghold that has three or four Siege markers.
 
B.4.4  Wastage (4.7.4)
  Seljuks then Romans must select and discard any one Asset
  or "This Lord" Capability card from each Lord who has more
  than one of any type of Asset or more than one such card.
 
B.4.5  Reset (4.7.5)
  Prepare for the next Turn:
    - Roman Commander may retain Themata Service Markers
      and their units from a Thema on his mat by paying 1 Coin
      per Thema with units present. If not, return each unpaid
      Themata marker to its home Thema and remove the
      provided Forces (1.6).
    - Unstack any Lieutenants and Lower Lords (4.1.3).
    - Discard all "This Campaign" Events (3.1.3).
    - Seljuk then Roman player may discard any Arts of War
      cards back into their decks (1.9.1).
    - Advance Campaign marker to the next Seasonal Turn box
      and flip the marker to Levy (2.2.2).
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
B.5  WINTER (4.7.6)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Conduct this phase after Reset in the first three AUTUMN turns
only (Calendar boxes 3, 6, and 9).
 
B.5.1  Aleppo Independence Victory Check
  If the ALEPPO INDEPENDENCE event has been played for its
  effect AND Aleppo is Seljuk Conquered, the Seljuk player
  wins an automatic victory (5.2).
 
B.5.2  Bounty
  [ERRATA APPLIED — page 18, 4.7.6]
  A Seljuk Lord who can trace a path of Locales that are
  Ruins, Wilderness, Unfortified Settlements, Friendly
  Strongholds free of enemy Lords, or Bypassed or Besieged
  Enemy Strongholds back to his Seat may score 1 VP per Loot
  on his Lord mat up to the number of Carts he and co-located
  Lords currently possess.
  Carts do not need to be assigned to a Way; their number just
  limits the amount of Loot that can be scored.
  Scored Loot tokens go into the Mosul & Baghdad Holding Box
  to track those VPs.
 
B.5.3  Seljuk Unity
  Count total Locales with Seljuk-colored Ruins, Conquered,
  or Ravaged markers (not VP value) on the map. Compare to
  the yearly Seljuk Unity value on the Calendar (Autumn box).
  If the number of Locales with those markers is LESS than the
  Calendar value, the Seljuk player loses VPs equal to the
  difference, removing Loot in Mosul & Baghdad first (if any),
  then placing Roman Conquered 1 VP markers in the
  Constantinople Holding Box. These Roman Conquered 1 VP
  markers are removed first when the Seljuk player scores
  positive VPs during a future Bounty phase.
 
B.5.4  Winter Quarters
  Unless allowed by a Capability to ignore this phase, return
  all Lords to their Seats Unladen by discarding any excess
  Assets (1.7.2). If a Lord's Seat is Enemy Conquered, place
  Lord in their Allied Holding Box. Each Lord then reduces
  his Carts to half their number, rounded up.
  Remove any MARWANID ALLIANCE Seat markers from map.
 
B.5.5  Aleppo Diplomacy
  If the Independent Aleppo marker is currently on the map,
  roll a die. On 1-2, remove the marker. On 3-6, it remains.
 
 
================================================================
C. VICTORY (5.0)
================================================================
C.1  Earning Victory Points (5.1)
  Track VPs on the Calendar (2.2.5).
    - Each side earns 1/2 VP per Ruins marker of its color.
    - Each side earns 1/2 VP per Ravaged marker of its color.
    - Roman player earns 1 VP per Roman Conquered marker
      on the map.
  Add to each side's total:
    - Roman: 1 VP each for Strategic Objective markers and
      Conquered markers in the Constantinople Holding Box.
    - Seljuk: 1 VP each for Loot markers in the Mosul &
      Baghdad Holding Box.
 
C.2  Campaign Victory (5.2)
  If at any moment during Campaign a side has NO Mustered
  Lords on the map, the game ends immediately — the other
  side wins regardless of VPs.
  The Seljuk player wins in the first phase of Winter if they
  have Conquered Aleppo and the ALEPPO INDEPENDENCE Event
  has been played for its effect.
 
C.3  End of Scenario Victory (5.3)
  If neither side wins by the Game End step (4.7.1) of the last
  Campaign, the side with more VPs wins. If tied, draw.
