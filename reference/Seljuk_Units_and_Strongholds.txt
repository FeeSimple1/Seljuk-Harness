storm_garrison_rules:
  reference: 4.9.1
  rules:
    - "Garrison Foot units have BOTH Melee and Missiles."
    - "Garrison Infantry and Militia are x1/2 Missile units with -1 to target Armor; Select Targets per 4.8.2."
    - "Garrison Strikes add to Defending Lord's Strikes (round up); Garrisons are otherwise separate from the Lord and ignore cards affecting the Lord individually."
    - "Defender must assign Hits to Garrison units first; the Lord's Forces absorb Hits only after Garrison is Routed."
    - "Garrison units return to the unit pool when Routed or at the end of the Storm."
    - "The full Garrison complement defends in every Enemy Storm action, regardless of past Storms at that Locale."
    - "Strongholds in the Fatimid Caliphate (Hama, Aleppo, Syria) always use the Seljuk Garrison column."
  R16_armenian_garrisons_capability:
    text: "Receive Garrison units from both sides of the Garrisons chart simultaneously."
    fort_verification: "Roman Fort (1 Militia) + Seljuk Fort (1 Infantry + 1 Militia) = 2 Militia + 1 Infantry combined."
