NOTES
================================================================
 
Whole-card routes (4)
  These four Ways consume an entire Command card to March across.
  Three are roads and one is a pass:
    Western Anatolia  <-> To Constantinople        (road)
    Ankyra            <-> To Constantinople        (road)
    Nusaybin          <-> To Mosul and Baghdad     (road)
    Vaspurakan        <-> To Mosul and Baghdad     (pass)
 
Lord seats by locale
  Regular Seats (per each Lord's mat):
    To Constantinople  : Romanos Diogenes, Manuel Komnenos,
                         Andronikos Doukas, Joseph Tarchaneiotes,
                         Nikephoros Bryennios
    Ankyra             : Roussel de Bailleul    (Roman alignment)
    Antioch            : Chatatourios
    Edessa             : Robert Crepin          (Roman alignment)
    Hama               : Ibn Khan
    Ani                : Alp Arslan, Afsin Beg, Sav-Tekin,
                         Emir of Arran, Sanduq al-Turki
    To Mosul and Baghdad : Alp Arslan, Afsin Beg, Arisighi,
                           Artuk Beg
 
  Conditional Seats:
    To Constantinople (when Roman-aligned) : Arisighi
    Amid          (via Marwanid Alliance, Seljuk Capability S8) :
                   all 8 Seljuk Lords
    Mayyafariqin  (via Marwanid Alliance, Seljuk Capability S8) :
                   all 8 Seljuk Lords
    To Mosul and Baghdad (when Seljuk-aligned and Roman Seat is
                          enemy-occupied) :
                   Robert Crepin, Roussel de Bailleul
 
  Notes:
    - Marwanid Alliance: marked on the map with the Kurdish star
      on Amid and Mayyafariqin. The Seljuk player spends a Coin
      (from Alp Arslan or from the Capability card itself) to
      activate one of the two Locales as a Seat until the end of
      the next Winter Phase (rule 3.5.1.1). Lords may not Tax
      these Locales while activated, and only one may serve as
      Supply Source per Command card.
    - Robert Crepin and Roussel de Bailleul are dual-allegiance
      Lords with Roman primary Seats. If their Seat is occupied
      by an enemy Lord when they would Muster, they use their
      current side's Holding Box instead (rule 1.4.2).
    - Arisighi is a dual-allegiance Lord with Seljuk primary
      Seats. When he defects to the Roman side, his Seat is
      always the Constantinople Holding Box, not a map Locale.
    - Artuk Beg has a unique Capability (S10 Artukid Legacy)
      that lets him treat Mosul and Baghdad as a Seat for
      Supply Routes and Bounty even without Marwanid Alliance
      in effect. This affects him only.
 
Locale types (per Rules 1.3.1)
  City      : 3-value stronghold
  Town      : 2-value stronghold
  Fort      : 1-value stronghold
  Unfortified settlement : not a stronghold; provides Provender
                           on Ravage; does not block Loot return
                           paths during Bounty
  Wilderness             : same Ravage/Bounty behavior as
                           Unfortified
  Holding box            : special movement and Muster rules;
                           Seat Locale for some Lords (no Enemy
                           Lord may enter)
 
Gardens
  Strongholds noted "with garden" aid Forage (Rule 4.5.4).
  Towns with garden: Amaseia, Neokaisareia, Ankyra, Ikonion,
    Kaisareia, Tarsos, Sebasteia, Melitene, Edessa, Germanikeia,
    Keltzene, Theodosiopolis, Romanoupolis, Nusaybin, Amid
  Cities with garden: Antioch, Aleppo, Ani
 
Allegiance (Rule 1.3.1)
  Roman (purple)  : all 15 themata's Locales plus To Constantinople
  Seljuk (blue)   : all Sultanate Locales plus To Mosul and Baghdad
  Fatimid (green) : Hama, Aleppo, Syria — Friendly to Roman with
                    several exceptions per the Fatimid Caliphate
                    rules; use Seljuk Garrison column on Storm
