SELJUK ARTS OF WAR REFERENCE
Source: Seljuk Playbook (Background Book), pp. 24-35, paired with card text.
Each card has a top half (Event, no Lord indicator) and a bottom half (Capability, with Lord indicator).
Clarifications from the playbook are integrated and trump the printed card text where they conflict.
An asterisk (*) on a card name denotes a once-per-game / specially-tracked card.

ROMAN CARDS
================================================================
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
R1. FLOODED RIVER CROSSING* / IMPERIAL FORTRESS CONSTRUCTION
----------------------------------------------------------------
EVENT - Flooded River Crossing*
Alp Arslan immediately undergoes Wastage 3 times (mark).
If already marked, 1 Seljuk Lord -1 Lordship this Muster (Seljuk choice) instead.
 
Clarification. The Seljuk player decides on the Wastage. If after executing a Wastage on Alp Arslan he does not qualify for further Wastage (because he only has 1 of each Asset or Capability), the Event effect ends; he is never forced to remove an Asset if he only has a single Asset of each type or Capability card. When using the "if already marked" effect, the selected Lord must be one able to Muster this turn, if possible.
 
CAPABILITY - Imperial Fortress Construction (Any)
Put 2 Fort markers on this card. As a Command action, this Lord may place a Fort marker on an unfortified Locale (including Ruined) in the Roman Empire (1.3.1).
 
Clarification. The Fort markers are placed when the Capability is Levied. The Lord does not have to be at the Locale in which he uses this card to place a Fort. A Seljuk Ruins marker is removed from the Locale when a Fort marker is placed (adjust VP). If both Fort markers are on the map, this Capability has no effect.
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
R2. MOUNTAIN AMBUSH / OGHUZ MERCENARIES
----------------------------------------------------------------
EVENT - Mountain Ambush
HOLD: May play in Battle if that Locale is adjacent to Pass. Round 1, Roman Walls 1-3 against Missiles.
 
Clarification. A Locale is adjacent to a Pass if one of its connected Ways is a Pass. The map's two holding boxes do not qualify as Pass even though March from them takes the entire card.
 
CAPABILITY - Oghuz Mercenaries (Any)
Add the Oghuz Mercenaries special Vassal to this Lord's mat.
 
Clarification. After spending the Lordship to Muster this Capability and place the special Vassal marker in the Assets section of his Lord mat, it takes an additional Lordship to Muster the Vassal to the Lord's Forces and receive the unit. If this card is discarded, remove the Vassal Force(s).
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
R3. SUMMER HEAT / STEELED RESOLVE
----------------------------------------------------------------
EVENT - Summer Heat
HOLD: Play in Summer after Seljuks reveal Command card. That Lord is Command 1.
 
Clarification. This effect takes place before any Command actions are performed after revealing the Command card. This card does not prevent Commands that cost an entire card like Tax, March from a holding box, etc.
 
CAPABILITY - Steeled Resolve (Any)
This Lord's Infantry have Armor 1-4 vs. Horse in any 1 Round of Battle (mark).
 
Clarification. The Armor 1-4 effect applies to both Missile and Melee. Account for Hits from the Javelins Capability separately from Horse Missiles during Battle when using this Capability. Use the Capability's marker to indicate this card's effect.
 
[CORRECTION (D-002, 2026-06-09): both Armor values above were transcribed as "1-3"; corrected to "1-4". Derived from the Player Aid Forces table (Infantry base Armor 1-3, confirmed by Eric) plus the no-op argument (a Capability granting the value Infantry already have would be a dead card half), NOT from published errata. If official Errata later addresses R3, the Errata file takes precedence over this derived correction. Residual check: confirm the printed R3 card reads "1-4"; if it reads "1-3", the no-op exists in the published game and is an upstream errata candidate, but the engine implements 1-4 regardless.]

R4. SULTAN'S HORSE IS KILLED / PRESSED LEVY SERVICE
----------------------------------------------------------------
EVENT - Sultan's Horse Is Killed
HOLD: Play where Alp Arslan Besieging Stronghold with >1 Siege marker: remove 1 Siege from Locale. OR play during Storm to reduce number of Rounds by 1.
 
Clarification. If used for the first effect, this card is played during Campaign when it is the Roman player's turn. If using for the second effect, it is played during the Play Events step of Storm. For either choice, the card can only be played on a Locale that has more than 1 Siege marker where Alp Arslan is located.
 
CAPABILITY - Pressed Levy Service (ALL)
Romans may keep 1 Unpaid Thema's Levied Themata with Lord at end of each Campaign.
 
Clarification. The Lord may keep all Levied Themata Service Markers from the chosen Thema at no cost. This Capability is not attached to any Lord and is placed at the board edge. Use the Capability marker to indicate this card's effect.

R5. FATIMID CONFLICT / NORMAN HEAVY CAVALRY
----------------------------------------------------------------
EVENT - Fatimid Conflict
Shift any 1 Seljuk cylinder or Service of Unbesieged Lord 1 Calendar box.
 
Clarification. This shift can be in any direction, Roman player's choice.
 
CAPABILITY - Norman Heavy Cavalry (Robert Crépin and Roussel de Bailleul)
This Lord may reroll 1 Norman Knights' Armor each Missiles AND Melee step.
 
Clarification. The reroll may be used on a different unit in Missiles and Melee. It is two rerolls: one during Missiles, and one during either Horse or Infantry Melee (not both).

----------------------------------------------------------------
EVENT - Michael Attaleiates
HOLD: Play on Romanos during Muster for Lordship +1.
 
Clarification. This card is played at any time during the Roman Muster phase.
 
CAPABILITY - Centralized Administration (Any, except Chatatourios, Robert Crépin, and Roussel de Bailleul)
This Lord beginning his activation inside the Roman Empire with a Supply Route gets +1 Command.
 
Clarification. "Inside the Roman Empire" means beginning his activation at a Locale to the west of the border with the Sultanate, regardless of who controls that Locale. This +1 Command applies even if that Lord leaves the Roman Empire during his activation. Supply Routes are defined in 4.4.1.

----------------------------------------------------------------
EVENT - Basil Alousianos Tricks the Sultan*
HOLD: Play as Roman Stronghold Surrenders on roll of 3 or less. Do not place Conquered; replace all Siege with Bypass (mark).
If marked, discard and draw new event.
 
Clarification. "Surrender on 3 or less" means the die roll needed to cause a Surrender (i.e., the Locale has some combination of 3 or more Siege and Ravaged markers). This Event cannot be played if there is a combination of four Siege + Ravaged markers at the Locale.
 
CAPABILITY - Emperor's Retinue (Romanos Diogenes)
Add the Emperor's Retinue special Vassal to this Lord's mat.
Vassal contents: 1x Varangian Guard, 1x Hetaireia, Service 1.
 
Clarification. After spending the Lordship to Muster this Capability and place the special Vassal marker in the Assets section of his Lord mat, it takes an additional Lordship to Muster the Vassal to the Lord's Forces and receive the units. If this card is discarded, remove the Vassal Force(s).

R8. ROMAN PERSUASION / MARTIAL SOCIETY
----------------------------------------------------------------
EVENT - Roman Persuasion
Add your Treachery card to this Season's Command Deck during Plan phase (4.1). Seljuk must add 1 No Command.
 
Clarification. The Treachery card is in addition to the normal Command cards called for by the Season. If both players add their Treachery card in the same turn, both players also still add a No Command to the deck (meaning there will be two extra cards: the Treachery and a No Command) in each.
 
CAPABILITY - Martial Society (Robert Crépin and Roussel de Bailleul)
This Lord is Command +1.
 
Clarification. This effect only lasts while the card is Mustered as a Capability.

----------------------------------------------------------------
EVENT - Winter Campaign
HOLD: Play in Winter before Bounty to activate a Roman Lord.
 
Clarification. The Roman Lord may be Besieged. If both players play Winter Campaign / Winter March at the same time, the Seljuk player goes first, followed by the Roman player.
 
CAPABILITY - Provincial Bureaucracy (Any Commander: Romanos Diogenes or Manuel Komnenos)
This Lord's first Tax Command outside Constantinople each Campaign does not place Ravaged.
 
Clarification. The Lord may Tax the same Locale a second time on a future Command card this Season, but if it is not his Seat, it will place a Ravaged marker.

R14. ALEPPO INDEPENDENCE / IMPERIAL COFFERS
----------------------------------------------------------------
EVENT - Aleppo Independence
Unless Aleppo Conquered (by either side), it is Friendly to both sides (mark), but neither may Withdraw inside. Aleppo can now yield Seljuk auto victory (5.2).
 
Clarification. If Aleppo is already Conquered when this Event is revealed, the Event has no effect. In this instance, this Event does not trigger the ability for the Seljuk player to win the game during Winter for holding Aleppo.
 
CAPABILITY - Imperial Coffers (ALL)
Discard during Arts of War if Roman adjacent to Seljuk-allied Robert or Roussel: make 1 Loyalty Check against that Lord.
May not be Levied if neither is Seljuk-allied.
 
Clarification. Any Roman Lord may be the one adjacent in order to discard this Capability for its effect. Discard this card after both players have drawn their Events in the Arts of War phase. Discard this card for its effect only after both players have resolved their Events. This Capability is not attached to any Lord and is placed at the board edge.

R15. EMIR OF ALEPPO SPURNS ALP ARSLAN* / FEALTY TO THE BASILEUS
----------------------------------------------------------------
EVENT - Emir of Aleppo Spurns Alp Arslan*
If Aleppo Seljuk Conquered and no Siege marker, place Independent Aleppo. If already present, place Roman Conquered instead. Adjust VP (mark).
If already marked (or no event effect), discard and draw new Event instead.
 
Clarification. The Event has no effect (discard and draw a new one) if any of the following is true: an Enemy Seljuk Lord is present in Aleppo when this Event is revealed; Aleppo is Roman Conquered. "If already present" means the Independent Aleppo marker is already on the map. Once this Event occurs, either by placing the Independent Aleppo marker or causing Aleppo to become Roman Conquered, its once-per-game effect is considered triggered (mark the Calendar) and it cannot occur again.
 
CAPABILITY - Fealty to the Basileus (Any)
This Lord may choose not to return to Seat in Winter.
 
Clarification. The Lord may stay in his current Locale, even if Besieging a Stronghold. If he chooses to return, he may not return to another Lord's Seat, another Locale, etc.

R16. ANGLO-SAXON EXILES / ARMENIAN GARRISONS
----------------------------------------------------------------
EVENT - Anglo-Saxon Exiles
If available, add 1 Varangian Guard unit to any Roman Lord mat (not Robert or Roussel) if he has no Varangian Guard.
 
Clarification. If neither of the two Varangian Guard are available (because both units are already with a Lord), or there are no Roman Lords who are not Robert or Roussel in play, this Event has no effect.
 
CAPABILITY - Armenian Garrisons (ALL)
All Roman-conquered Strongholds outside Roman Empire (1.3.1) use both Seljuk and Roman Garrison units.
 
Clarification. Read this literally: when generating Garrison units, receive the units from both sides of the Garrisons chart (e.g., if a Fort, receive 2 Militia and 1 Infantry). This Capability is not attached to any Lord and is placed at the board edge.

This Campaign, if Spring or Autumn, Passes cannot be used for Supply, March, Avoid Battle, or Retreat. If Summer, Plan stack (4.1) is 9 cards. Seljuk player must use 1 No Command.

R18. LOCAL SCOUTS / ICON OF THE VIRGIN MARY
----------------------------------------------------------------
EVENT - Local Scouts
HOLD: Play as Enemy Avoids Battle. Enemy chooses 1 Avoiding Lord to Battle or Withdraw instead.
 
Clarification. The other Enemy Lords not selected must follow through with the original Avoid Battle decision. Choosing Withdraw may only occur at a Friendly Stronghold (except a Friendly Aleppo). Resolve the Avoid Battle before Battle begins.
 
CAPABILITY - Icon of the Virgin Mary (Any)
Discard this card when calculating Losses after Battle: this Lord's routed units recover per armor, regardless of situation.
 
Clarification. "Regardless of situation" means ignoring any penalties to Losses after Battle, the effects of other cards, or whether or not the Lord discarding the Capability Conceded the Field. Use of this card always guarantees the Lord may roll against Routed units' Protection.

R19. RESILIENT AGRICULTURE / NOMISMA DEBASED*
----------------------------------------------------------------
EVENT - Resilient Agriculture
Remove 2 Ravaged Markers from the Roman Empire (1.3.1).
 
Clarification. Only Seljuk Ravaged markers may be removed. The Roman player may not choose Ravaged markers in the Fatimid Caliphate. Remove as many as possible, and removal is mandatory.
 
CAPABILITY - Nomisma Debased* (ALL)
Discard during Pay step of Levy to shift all Roman Service 1 Calendar box. Non-Commander Roman Lords may not Tax for rest of game (mark).
 
Clarification. This Capability can only be triggered once per game. The Roman player may pay none, some, or all of their Lords with Coin or Loot before discarding this card for its effect. Commander Lords (Romanos Diogenes and Manuel Komnenos) are exempt from the Tax prohibition and may continue to Tax. This Capability is not attached to any Lord and is placed at the board edge.

R20. ARMENIAN RESISTANCE / SIEGE WEAPONRY
----------------------------------------------------------------
EVENT - Armenian Resistance
If Khliat, Manzikert, or Arkesh is Seljuk friendly, choose one and place Roman Conquered Marker. Adjust VP.
 
Clarification. If the chosen listed Locale has a Seljuk Lord present, the Seljuk player immediately places a Siege or Bypass marker there (Seljuk choice). If there is a Strategic Objective marker at this Locale, return it to available (do not place it in Constantinople as VP).
 
CAPABILITY - Siege Weaponry (Any, except Robert Crépin and Roussel de Bailleul)
This Lord unrouted at Storm reduces Enemy Walls -1.
 
Clarification. This effect only persists while the Lord is Unrouted; if he becomes Routed during the Storm, enemy Walls return to their normal value. This Capability is also in effect if the Lord is in Reserve.
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
R21. NOMADIC TRIBES GO ROGUE / BARDOUKIA
----------------------------------------------------------------
EVENT - Nomadic Tribes Go Rogue
HOLD: Play before Battle or Storm to remove up to 2 Turkic Horse units at the Active Lord's Locale from play.
 
Clarification. The removed units are eliminated immediately, with no roll to recover as after Battle. This event can affect a Besieged Lord, or a Lord not taking part in the Battle. This can occur to a Lord who only has two Turkic Horse units, thereby causing a Disband.
 
CAPABILITY - Bardoukia (Any)
This Lord's Tagmata inflict -1 to target armor during each Melee Strike step of Battle.
 
Clarification. The recipient of Bardoukia attacks is advised to roll Protection against these hits separately from other hits during the same Melee step.
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
R22. ASSASSINATION PLOT IN TYRE / CAVALRY SUPPLY LINE RAIDERS
----------------------------------------------------------------
EVENT - Assassination Plot in Tyre
Unless Ibn Khan within 2 Locales of Aleppo, permanently Disband him. Otherwise, shift his Service 1 Calendar box.
 
Clarification. Being 2 Locales away from Aleppo is considered "within" (for example, Manbij, Artah, Laodikeia, and Jazira). This shift can be in any direction, Roman player's choice, and may cause Disband. Having only Turkic Horse does not help to be considered closer.
 
CAPABILITY - Cavalry Supply Line Raiders (Any)
During Wastage, 1 Enemy Lord adjacent to This Lord undergoes Wastage twice.
 
Clarification. The second occurrence of Wastage caused by this card does not happen if after the first Wastage procedure, the enemy Lord does not qualify for triggering Wastage (i.e., they only have a single Asset of all types on their Lord mat). In the case of multiple adjacent Lords, the Roman player chooses which Lord suffers the Wastage.
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
R23. KLEISOURAI / ALAKATIA
----------------------------------------------------------------
EVENT - Kleisourai
HOLD: Play when Seljuk Lord Marches, Avoids Battle, or Retreats across Pass. Each moving Seljuk Lord takes 1 Hit (as if in Battle, no Losses roll).
 
Clarification. The Seljuk player still gets to roll Protection for the unit assigned the Hit, but if the Protection roll fails, the unit is eliminated with no roll to recover as after a Battle.
 
CAPABILITY - Alakatia (Any)
This Lord with 2 Infantry in Battle adds 1 extra Missile Hit with -1 to target armor.
 
Clarification. The defender should roll the extra Hit from Alakatia separately in most cases since it has the same special anti-armor effect as Garrison Missiles. If during a subsequent round of the same Battle the Lord has less than 2 Unrouted Infantry, he does not strike with the extra Hit (i.e., the Capability does not take effect). A Lord with more than 2 Infantry does not generate extra Hits (e.g., 4 Infantry does not cause 2 Hits).
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
R24. CAVALRY CHARGE / MULES
----------------------------------------------------------------
EVENT - Cavalry Charge
HOLD: Play on 1 Roman Lord in Battle. Round 1, that Lord's Horse Melee precedes all Missiles.
 
Clarification. The Horse units that Melee Strike before Missiles skip their usual Melee Strike phase (after Missiles) that round. If this card is played at the same time as Command Confusion, Cavalry Charge takes precedence.
 
CAPABILITY - Mules (Any)
This Lord's first March across Pass spends only 1 Command even if Laden.
 
Clarification. This effect applies once each Command card. This effect does not occur for this Lord if stacked under a Lieutenant or being moved by a Commander as part of a Group March.
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
R25. HONORS OF WAR / PSILOI
----------------------------------------------------------------
EVENT - Honors of War
HOLD: Play during Siege on Fort; instead of rolling, Fort automatically Surrenders. Take no Spoils.
 
Clarification. The active Lord must still take a Siege action in order for this card to be played.
 
CAPABILITY - Psiloi (ALL)
Roman Militia have Missiles x½.
 
Clarification. The effect of this card applies to all non-garrison Roman Militia in play (Roman Garrison militia already have Missile x½ with -1 to Armor as default). This Capability is not attached to any Lord and is placed at the board edge.
