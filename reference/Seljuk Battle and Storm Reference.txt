================================================================
SELJUK — BATTLE AND STORM PROCEDURE REFERENCE
================================================================
Source: Rules of Play sections 4.8-4.9, cross-checked against
Seljuk_Errata_and_Clarifications.txt and the Playbook examples.
Errata corrections applied inline.
 
PRECEDENCE
  Where this file and the Rules of Play or the Errata &
  Clarifications differ, the Rules and the Errata govern.
 
CONVENTIONS
  "Attacker" = the Marching side that triggered the Battle.
  "Defender" = the side already at the Locale.
  Unit stats (Strike values, Protection ranges) are in the
  Units and Strongholds reference file; this document covers
  only the procedural flow.
  All dice are standard d6.
 
 
================================================================
1. BATTLE (4.8)
================================================================
TRIGGER: When a Lord Approaches (4.3.4) an Unbesieged,
Unbypassed Enemy Lord who does not Avoid Battle or Withdraw,
a Battle ensues. All Unbesieged and Unbypassed Lords in the
Locale must participate; others may not.
NOTE: Storm (4.9) follows similar but distinct rules from
field Battle.
 
MARKER: Place the Battle/Storm marker at the Locale.
 
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
1.1  BATTLE ARRAY (4.8.1)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Players Array their Lords — cylinders on the Battle mat or
mats on the table, as preferred.
 
POSITIONING ORDER:
  1. Attackers position their Lords.
  2. Defenders position their Lords.
 
FRONT ROW (up to 3 positions: Left, Center, Right):
  - The Active Lord (whose Command card was revealed) MUST
    start at Front Center.
  - Attackers then fill Front Left and/or Right with one other
    Lord in each, if any.
  - Remaining Attacking Lords go in Reserve.
  - The Defender MUST put one Lord directly opposite each
    Front Attacking Lord, filling Center first, then Left and/or
    Right, as able.
  - Remaining Defending Lords go in Reserve.
 
EVENTS:
  Attacking then Defending sides may play Held Events that
  specify "in Battle" or "in Storm", as applicable (3.1.3).
  NOTE: If CAVALRY CHARGE and COMMAND CONFUSION are
  both played in the same Battle, CAVALRY CHARGE takes
  precedence.
 
RELIEF SALLY (4.8.1):
  When a side Approaches a Locale where it is also Besieged,
  any Besieged Lords may join the Attack for no additional
  Command actions.
  - Array Sallying Attackers in a row behind the Defenders.
  - Any Defending Lords in Reserve instead position opposite
    Sallying Attackers as a Rearguard row.
  - If no Rearguard, Sallying Lords fight Front Defenders as
    if Flanking them all equally closely (4.8.2).
  - Siegeworks (4.9.1) protect against Strikes by Sallying
    Attackers only (round separately).
  - If Attackers lose, Withdraw Sallying Lords back into the
    Stronghold (4.8.3) and reduce Siege markers there to one
    (4.9.2).
 
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
1.2  ROUNDS (4.8.2)
----------------------------------------------------------------
A Battle continues Round after Round until a side Concedes or
all its Lords Rout. Each Round proceeds:
  Step 1: Concede the Field?
  Step 2: Reposition
  Step 3: Strike (Missile, then Melee)
NOTE: Some Battle Event effects vary by Round.
 
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
1.2.1  CONCEDE THE FIELD? (4.8.2)
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
At the start of each Round, Attacker then Defender may declare
the Battle will end after this Round with that side as the loser
but with diminished losses (4.8.3-4).
NOTE: Battles last at least one Round (no Conceding in the
first Round).
 
PURSUIT MARKER: Place the Pursuit marker between the two
sides, pointing toward the Conceding side. The Conceding side
halves its Hits in this final Round (see Strike below).
 
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
1.2.2  REPOSITION (4.8.2)
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
In each Round AFTER the first, both sides reposition Lords in
this order:
 
  A. ROUT: Remove Routed Lords from the Battle Array. They
     no longer occupy any position nor participate further until
     Ending the Battle (4.8.3).
 
  B. ADJUST ROWS (Relief Sally only): If all of any row
     Routed:
       - If no Sallying Lords remain, Rearguard becomes
         Reserve.
       - If no Rearguard, Sallying Lords Flank Defenders.
       - If no Front Defenders, Rearguard faces about as
         Reserve.
 
  C. ADVANCE LORDS: Attacker then Defender may slide any
     Unrouted Lords in Reserve into any empty Front positions
     (one each).
 
  D. CENTER: If a center position remains empty, first the
     Attacker then the Defender must select and slide one of
     that side's Lords from Left or Right Front to fill the
     empty Front Center.
 
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
1.2.3  STRIKE (4.8.2)
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
Forces of each Lord Strike those of the Lord directly opposite,
or if Flanking, the closest enemy Lord in that row.
 
INITIATIVE — Strike steps proceed in this fixed order. Each
lettered step is resolved fully (Hits, Protection, Rout) before
the next step:
 
  1. MISSILE STEPS
     a. Defending Missiles
     b. Attacking Missiles
 
  2. MELEE STEPS
     a. Defending Horse
     b. Attacking Horse
     c. Defending Foot
     d. Attacking Foot
 
Within each step, the Striking side chooses the order of
Strike Lord by Lord (or Lord with Flanking Lord[s]). Then any
Flanking Lords choose whether to absorb Strikes before
opposed Lords. Resolve all Hits, Protection, and Rout before
proceeding to the next step.
 
FLANKING:
  A Lord facing an enemy row with no enemy Lord directly
  opposite Flanks and Strikes the closest enemy Lord in that
  row. A Lord in Center Flanks Left and Right and chooses one
  to Strike.
  Total all Hits from Flanking Lords plus the directly opposed
  Lord, then round up.
  A Flanking Lord may absorb Hits from a Lord he Flanks if no
  enemies Flank the target Lord.
 
TOTAL HITS:
  Each unit causes 1/2, 1, 2, or 3 Hits depending on Forces type
  and Strike type — see the Forces table. Total all Hits for that
  step, including Flanking plus directly opposing Lords, round
  up. NOTE: Hits are automatic based on Forces; you do not
  roll to generate Hits.
 
  MIXED MISSILES: When Garrison Missiles or Missiles that
  choose targets (PARTHIAN SHOT Capability) combine with
  normal Missiles, round Hits in favor of the Garrison or
  Capability Missiles.
 
  PURSUIT: The Conceding side halves its total Hits against
  the Pursuing side. Round all fractions up within each step.
  EXCEPTION: A single Conceding Lord with ONLY Turkic
  Horse units in his Forces at the start of the battle, facing
  only a single opposing Lord, causes BOTH sides to be halved.
 
APPLY HITS TO LORDS:
  Hits apply to the Forces of the opposed, Flanked, or Flanking
  Enemy Lord. A player with a Flanking Lord where no enemies
  are Flanking the target chooses either the Flanking or
  directly opposed Lord to take Hits.
  Whenever a Lord Routs to create a new Flanking situation,
  apply remaining Hits accordingly.
 
ROLL WALLS (Storm/Sally only):
  A Lord benefiting from any Walls or any Siege markers first
  rolls dice equal to the total Hits just received in that step
  BEFORE assigning any Hits to units.
  Each roll within the Walls range specified (typically 1-4) or
  less than or equal to the total Siege markers cancels one Hit.
  Roll Hits that differ from others (such as -2 Armor) separately.
 
ASSIGN HITS TO UNITS:
  After rolling for any Walls, the owner chooses which unit
  absorbs each Hit, Hit by Hit.
  EXCEPTIONS:
    - Select Target: Garrison Missiles and PARTHIAN SHOT
      allow the Striking side to select which enemy unit takes
      each such Hit. The player receiving Hits first chooses a
      Lord to be affected (when Flanking), then the Striking
      side Selects Target units for those Hits; the owner
      chooses units for any other Hits.
    - Storm Attack: Hits against a side Attacking in Storm
      must be applied to Armored before Unarmored units,
      regardless of who is choosing (4.9.1).
  IMPORTANT: A unit may absorb several Hits in succession,
  shielding fellow units, as long as it succeeds on each
  Protection roll.
 
ROLL PROTECTION BY HIT:
  Units roll Protection as follows:
 
  ARMOR: A unit with any Armor Protection rolls a die. A roll
  within the Armor range specified (Forces table or card) means
  the Hit has no effect (no Rout).
 
  EVADE: Units with Evade Protection roll and absorb Hits
  just as if Armored, EXCEPT Evade is NOT used against:
    - Missile Hits, OR
    - Hits in Storm.
 
  GARRISON MISSILES: Missile Hits from Stronghold Garrisons
  roll against Armor reduced by -1, to a minimum Armor of "1".
 
  UNARMORED: Militia avoid Routing on a Hit only on a roll
  of 1.
 
ROUT:
  A unit is Routed as soon as it fails to negate a Hit with a
  successful Protection roll. Slide it to the "Routed" section
  of the Lord's mat. Routed units no longer Strike nor absorb
  Hits in that Battle.
 
  A Lord Routs when his last Unrouted unit Routs. Remove him
  from the Array. A new Flanking situation may immediately
  result. When an entire row Routs, ignore remaining Hits
  against that row.
 
  Track Themata status by keeping their units on their marker.
  NOTE: When a Themata marker with more than one unit has
  only one unit Rout, track which units belong to the Themata
  until Battle ends.
 
NEW ROUND:
  If neither side Conceded AND at least one Lord on each side
  has yet to Rout, begin a new Round at "Concede the Field?"
  Otherwise, end per 1.3 below.
 
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
1.3  ENDING THE BATTLE (4.8.3)
----------------------------------------------------------------
A side that Conceded at the start of the Round OR that has no
Unrouted Lords (when the other side did not Concede) loses the
Battle at the end of that Round.
 
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
1.3.1  RETREAT, WITHDRAWAL, OR REMOVAL (4.8.3)
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
All losing Lords must either:
  A. RETREAT to a single adjacent Locale with no Enemy Lords
     or Strongholds that are not already Besieged or Bypassed
     (4.3.5), OR
  B. WITHDRAW into a Friendly Stronghold at the Battle Locale
     (if Sallied or is Defending at one), OR
  C. Be REMOVED per 4.8.5 below.
 
The owning player chooses each Lord's fate, within these
requirements:
  - Defenders may NOT Retreat along any part of the Way that
    Attackers used to Approach the Locale.
  - Marching Attackers MUST Retreat to the Locale whence
    they Approached (4.3.4).
    [ERRATA APPLIED — Playbook p.17: Attackers may NOT
    Withdraw into the Stronghold at the site of Battle because
    they are neither the Defender nor Sallying.]
  - Sallying Attackers MUST Withdraw back into their
    Stronghold.
  - Retreating Lords may join Friendly Lords already Bypassing
    an Enemy Stronghold (4.8.3 rules, Seljuk summary of
    changes).
 
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
1.3.2  LOSSES (4.8.4)
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
After losing Lords Retreat, Withdraw, or are removed, BOTH
sides determine the fate of their Routed units:
 
  Roll a die for each Routed unit:
 
  HARSH LOSSES (Protection = 1 only):
    - All Routed units of Lords who Retreated WITHOUT having
      Conceded the Field (4.8.2), OR
    - Who were Attacking in Storm (4.9.1)
    ...fail unless they roll a "1".
 
  NORMAL LOSSES:
    - For Routed units of all other Lords (even if the Lord
      Routed after Conceding the Field), compare each unit's
      roll to its inherent Protection as shown on the Forces
      table, UNMODIFIED by Events or Capabilities.
 
  RESULTS:
    - Units that roll within Protection range: no longer
      Routed — push above the line on the mat. (Service
      Markers stay put.)
    - Units that fail: Lost — remove to pool.
    - Once a Themata Service Marker has all its units Lost in
      Battle, the Service Marker is removed from the game.
    - For a Lord who loses ALL his Forces in Battle or Storm,
      remove him per 4.8.5 below.
 
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
1.3.3  SPOILS (4.8.3)
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
Lords on the losing side may have to immediately transfer
Assets to Lords on the winning side. The winning player
distributes these Assets among mats of Lords at the Locale:
 
  - Lords who WITHDREW: keep all their Assets.
  - Lords who CONCEDED and RETREATED: transfer all Loot
    and any Provender beyond that which they could move
    without being Laden (4.3.2). Lose no other Assets.
  - Losing Lords who were REMOVED (unable to Retreat or
    Withdraw, or by Losses), or who RETREATED WITHOUT
    HAVING CONCEDED the Field: transfer ALL their Assets.
 
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
1.3.4  SERVICE (4.8.3)
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
The losing side rolls one die for EACH of its Retreated Lords
and shifts that Lord's Service Marker (and, if using Vassal
Service optional rule 6.2, each of his Vassals' markers) left:
  - Roll 1, 2, or 3: shift left 1 box.
  - Roll 4, 5, or 6: shift left 2 boxes.
 
  - Losing Lords who WITHDREW into a Stronghold do NOT
    shift their Service.
 
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
1.3.5  LORD REMOVAL BY COMBAT (4.8.5)
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
Lords removed in Battle (4.8.3-4) or Storm (4.9.1) — by failure
to Retreat or Withdraw, Loss of all Forces, or Sack — suffer
removal as if Disbanding Beyond Service (3.3.1).
NOTE: Later Disband due to Service Limit (3.3) does not count
as removal by combat, even if Battle caused a shortening of
Service.
 
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
1.3.6  AFTERMATH (4.8.6)
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
After resolving Spoils and Losses:
 
  A. MOVED/FOUGHT: Mark all Attacking and Defending Lords
     Moved/Fought (if not already marked).
  B. EVENTS: Discard all Hold Events (3.1.3) used in this
     Battle or Storm.
  C. SIEGE: If the combat created or ended a Siege, adjust
     Siege, Conquered, Ruins, and/or Victory markers (1.3.1,
     4.3.5, 4.9.1, 5.1).
  D. RECOVERY: Skip any Command actions remaining on this
     card. Go to Feed/Pay/Disband (4.6).
 
  IMPORTANT: A Battle or Storm blocks any further Command
  actions on the current Command card.
 
 
================================================================
2. STORM (4.9.1)
================================================================
Any Lord outside a Besieged Stronghold may use a Command
action to launch an Attack. Proceed per Battle rules (4.8)
EXCEPT as modified below. Refer to the Battle and Storm chart.
NOTE: Some Events and Capabilities apply to Battle but not
Storm, or vice versa.
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
2.1  STORM ARRAY
----------------------------------------------------------------
Follow Battle rules (4.8.1-4.8.2) EXCEPT:
  - Each side's Front row begins with AT MOST ONE Lord (for
    the Attacker, the Active Lord).
  - All other Lords start in Reserve.
  - More Lords may later Reposition to the Front, up to the
    Stronghold's Size (1, 2, or 3).
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
2.2  STORM CONCEDE
----------------------------------------------------------------
At the start of each Round AFTER the first, the Attacker (only)
may end the Storm. The Attacker loses. Go to Ending the Storm.
The Defender may NOT Concede in a Storm.
NOTE: Neither side may Concede in the first Round.
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
2.3  STORM REPOSITION
----------------------------------------------------------------
In each Storm Round after the first, Attacker then Defender may
add one Lord from Reserve to the Front, up to Stronghold Size
(1, 2, or 3).
A Reserve Lord (if any present) MUST move to Front if all of
their Front Lords Routed, or MUST move to Front to match an
enemy Front Lord.
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
2.4  STRONGHOLD EFFECTS
----------------------------------------------------------------
GARRISON FORCES:
  - The Besieged side receives the Garrison units (and for
    Romans, any present Themata Service Markers chosen to
    defend the Stronghold at the start of the Siege) shown for
    that type of Stronghold on the Strongholds table.
  - Current Allegiance of the Stronghold determines which
    column (Roman or Seljuk) is used on the Strongholds
    player aid card.
  - Strongholds in the Fatimid Caliphate (1.3.1) always use
    the Seljuk Garrison column.
  - In the rare case the pool has too few pieces, do not place
    those units.
 
GARRISON FORCES DURING STORM:
  - Garrison Foot units have both Melee and Missiles.
  - Garrison Infantry and Militia are x1/2 Missile units that
    SELECT Enemy targets (4.8.2) with -1 to Enemy Armor.
  - Garrisons add their Strikes to those of the Defending Lord
    (rounding up), if any, but do not otherwise affect the Lord's
    Strikes. Garrisons are separate from any Defending Lord
    and ignore cards affecting that Lord individually.
  - The Defender MUST assign all Hits suffered to Garrison
    units until they are Routed, after which the Front Defending
    Lord's units (if any) absorb Hits.
  - Whenever Routed and at the end of the Storm, return
    Garrison units to the pool.
  - The full complement of Garrison units Defends in EACH
    enemy Storm action, regardless of what happened in
    previous Storm actions.
 
WALLS:
  The Besieged side uses that Stronghold's Walls per 4.8.2
  and the Strongholds table.
 
SIEGEWORKS:
  The Besieging side places all Siege markers at the Locale
  in front of its Attacking Lord(s). Those Lords during the
  Storm have Walls with Value equal to the number of Siege
  markers against both Missile and Melee Strikes.
  EXAMPLE: Lords Storming with 3 Siege markers benefit
  from Walls 1-3.
 
MELEE ORDER IN STORM:
  All Defending units Melee Strike BEFORE any Attacking
  units do, unlike Battle where Horse Melee precedes Foot
  (4.8.2). The Storm Strike sequence is:
 
  1. MISSILE STEPS
     a. Defending Missiles (including Garrison Missiles)
     b. Attacking Missiles
 
  2. MELEE STEPS
     a. Defending Horse Melee
     b. Defending Foot Melee
     c. Attacking Horse Melee
     d. Attacking Foot Melee
 
STORM ATTACK RESTRICTION:
  Hits against a side Attacking in Storm MUST be applied to
  Armored units before Unarmored units, regardless of who is
  choosing.
 
MELEE CAP:
  Each Lord of each side in Storm adds no more than 6 Hits in
  Melee. (Missiles are unlimited.)
 
EVADE RESTRICTION:
  Evade Protection is NOT used against Hits in Storm (see
  1.2.3 Protection rules above).
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
2.5  ENDING THE STORM
----------------------------------------------------------------
A Storm ends once the number of Rounds COMPLETED equals
the number of Siege markers there, or earlier if:
  - A side loses because all its Lords Rout, OR
  - The Attacker Concedes.
 
ATTACKER LOSES (Defenders NOT all Routed):
  - Losing Attackers neither Retreat nor give up Spoils.
  - The Locale remains Besieged — return Siege markers to
    the map and go to Aftermath (4.8.6).
 
DEFENDER LOSES:
  - Sack the Stronghold (see 2.6 below).
  - If Defenders win, any Themata Service Markers garrisoning
    the Stronghold remain.
 
LOSSES:
  Both sides' Forces take Losses per Battle (4.8.4), EXCEPT:
    - Routed Defending units always roll against their inherent
      Protection.
    - Routed Attacking units that fail to roll a "1" are removed.
 
MOVED/FOUGHT:
  Mark ALL Lords at the Storm Locale as Moved/Fought, even
  Lords who remained in Reserve.
  NOTE: Lords at a Storm Locale may not simply sit it out.
  Used or not, they showed up for action.
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
2.6  SACK (4.9.1)
----------------------------------------------------------------
When Besieged Defenders lose a Storm:
 
  A. CONQUER: The Besiegers Conquer the Stronghold. Remove
     Siege markers. Place or remove Conquered markers (1.3.1).
     EXCEPTION: In the Fatimid Caliphate, the Roman player
     only removes Seljuk Conquered markers and does not place
     their own.
     If the Stronghold now has a Ravaged marker that is the
     same color as its Allegiance printed on the map, flip it to
     the opposite color.
     If the Stronghold has a Roman Strategic Objective marker,
     the Roman player claims it in the Constantinople Holding
     Box.
     Adjust VP marker on the Calendar (2.2.5, 5.1).
 
  B. RUIN (Seljuk only, Roman Empire Strongholds only):
     Instead of Conquering, the Seljuk player may choose to
     Ruin it. Remove all Siege markers (1.3.1, 4.5.1). Place
     one Ruins marker. Adjust VPs (2.2.5, 5.1).
 
  C. AWARD SPOILS: Besieging Lords receive and distribute
     as desired among their mats a number of Loot, Provender,
     and Coin each according to the Stronghold's Value (1, 2,
     or 3; see Strongholds table) plus all Assets from
     Disbanding Lords.
     EXAMPLE: Sacking a Town yields 2 Provender and 1 Coin.
 
  D. REMOVE LORDS: Remove each losing Lord per 4.8.5 —
     Disband him as if Beyond Service (3.3.1).
 
  E. REMOVE THEMATA: Remove each losing Themata Service
     Marker (1.5.1), eliminating them from the game.
 
AFTERMATH: Proceed with Aftermath (4.8.6).
 
 
================================================================
3. SALLY (4.9.2)
================================================================
A Besieged Lord may use a Command action to Attack Besiegers
in a Battle. Follow Battle rules (4.8), including skipping any
remaining Command actions (4.8.6), EXCEPT:
 
ARRAY AND REPOSITION:
  Each side begins with one Lord in Front (for the Attacker,
  the Active Lord); other Lords start in Reserve. In each Round
  after the first, Attacker then Defender may add one Lord
  from Reserve to the Front, up to Stronghold Size (1, 2, or 3).
  If all Front Lords Routed, a Reserve Lord (if any present)
  must move to Front.
  NOTE: Sally positioning mimics Storm (4.9.1).
 
SIEGEWORKS:
  Defenders (the Besiegers being Sallied against) receive
  Siegeworks as if Storming (4.9.1).
  NOTE: Sallying Attackers do NOT receive any Walls or
  Garrison.
 
END:
  Losing Defenders (Besiegers) Retreat normally, ending the
  Siege.
  Losing Attackers (the Besieged Sallying force) must Withdraw
  back into their Stronghold (4.8.3, not Retreat).
 
RAID:
  If Sallying Attackers lose, remove all but one Siege marker
  at the Locale (reflecting damage to Siegeworks by the
  sortie). The Siege goes on.
 
 
================================================================
QUICK REFERENCE: BATTLE FLOW SUMMARY
================================================================
  1. Approach triggers Battle (4.3.4)
  2. Array Lords (4.8.1)
     - Attacker positions, then Defender
     - Play Held "in Battle" Events
  3. ROUND (repeat until Battle ends):
     a. Concede the Field? (Attacker, then Defender; not Round 1)
     b. Reposition (Rounds 2+: remove Routed, adjust rows,
        advance Reserve Lords, fill Center)
     c. Strike:
        i.   Defending Missiles → Hits → Protection → Rout
        ii.  Attacking Missiles → Hits → Protection → Rout
        iii. Defending Horse Melee → Hits → Protection → Rout
        iv.  Attacking Horse Melee → Hits → Protection → Rout
        v.   Defending Foot Melee → Hits → Protection → Rout
        vi.  Attacking Foot Melee → Hits → Protection → Rout
     d. New Round? (if neither Conceded and both sides have
        Unrouted Lords, go to 3a; else end)
  4. Ending the Battle (4.8.3)
     a. Retreat / Withdrawal / Removal
     b. Losses (both sides roll for Routed units)
     c. Spoils (losing side transfers Assets)
     d. Service (losing Retreated Lords shift left)
  5. Aftermath (4.8.6)
     a. Mark Moved/Fought
     b. Discard used Hold Events
     c. Adjust Siege/Conquered/Ruins/VP markers
     d. Skip remaining actions → Feed/Pay/Disband (4.6)
 
 
================================================================
QUICK REFERENCE: STORM FLOW SUMMARY
================================================================
  1. Lord uses Command action to Storm (4.5.2, 4.9.1)
  2. Array Lords (one per side in Front; rest in Reserve)
     - Place Siege markers as Siegeworks for Attacker
     - Place Walls markers for Defender
     - Defender receives Garrison units and any Themata
     - Play Held "in Storm" Events
  3. ROUND (repeat up to # of Siege markers):
     a. Concede? (Attacker only; not Round 1)
     b. Reposition (add 1 Lord per side to Front, up to
        Stronghold Size)
     c. Strike:
        i.   Defending Missiles (incl. Garrison) → Walls →
             Assign → Protection → Rout
        ii.  Attacking Missiles → Siegeworks → Assign
             (Armored first) → Protection (no Evade) → Rout
        iii. Defending Horse Melee → same
        iv.  Defending Foot Melee → same
        v.   Attacking Horse Melee (max 6 Hits/Lord) → same
        vi.  Attacking Foot Melee (max 6 Hits/Lord) → same
     d. New Round? (if Rounds completed < Siege markers and
        neither side fully Routed and Attacker did not Concede,
        go to 3a; else end)
  4. Ending the Storm (4.9.1)
     - If Defenders lose → Sack (Conquer or Ruin, Award
       Spoils, Remove Lords, Remove Themata)
     - If Attackers lose → no Retreat, no Spoils given; Siege
       continues; return Siege markers to map
     - Losses (Routed Attackers need "1"; Routed Defenders
       roll normal Protection)
  5. Aftermath (4.8.6)
     - All Lords at Locale marked Moved/Fought
