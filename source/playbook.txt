         From the Designer
         The asymmetry of SELJUK is one of its core tenets, and playing
         well means dictating the terms and pace of the game such that
         your opponent is reacting to you and not the other way around.

         fed and making the most of their activations will determine vic-
         tory and defeat.

         Pay
         Both players now move to the Pay phase. First the Seljuks have
         the option to pay Coin to their Lords to extend their Service.
         Neither Ibn Khan nor Arisighi have any Coin to be able to ex-
         tend their own Service. However, as a Commander (indicated
         by his taller cylinder and the symbol on his mat) on the map,
         Alp Arslan is allowed to pay Coin from the Assets area of his
         Lord mat for any Unbesieged Seljuk Lords, regardless of how
         far away he is. The Seljuk player decides to spend one Coin
         from Alp Arslan’s mat on Ibn Khan, moving Ibn Khan’s Service
         marker forward from the Autumn 1068 box on the calendar to
         the Spring 1069 box.
         The Roman player declines to Pay any of their Lords.

         Disband
         Looking at the calendar, if any Lord’s Service marker was in the
         current Calendar box or earlier than the turn marker, then those
         Lords would Disband and be placed on the Calendar (if on the
         current turn box) or removed from the game (if left of the cur-
         rent turn box). In Spring 1068, no such Lord meets these criteria.
